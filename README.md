# Poker Rogue

A Balatro-style poker-hands roguelike that runs entirely in your browser. Battle through 8 antes of Small, Big, and Boss blinds: build poker hands, score Chips × Mult, buy Jokers and Planet cards in the shop, and take down the boss blind to win the run.

## ▶ Play the game

**[Play Poker Rogue](https://inconstantsnap-commits.github.io/poker-rogue/)**

No install, no account — just open the link. Works on phone and desktop.

## How it plays

- Each blind shows a target score; you get 4 hands and 3 discards to reach it.
- Play up to 5 cards per hand — only the cards that form your poker hand score.
- Win dollars for clearing blinds, then spend them in the shop on stackable Jokers and Planet cards that permanently level up your hand types.
- Boss blinds have teeth: The Wall doubles the target, The Hook tears cards from your hand, and The Eye forbids repeating a hand type.
- Clear Ante 8 to win the run.
